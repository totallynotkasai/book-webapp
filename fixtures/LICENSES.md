# Fixture and demo content licences

Every file under `fixtures/` and `apps/web/static/` is listed below with its source and licence (DESIGN.md §12.2, task P0-11). **Every source is public domain or CC0.** When you add, replace or remove a file in either folder, update this table in the same commit.

## Rules we follow

- **Public domain in both the UK and the US.** UK copyright lasts 70 years after the author's or artist's death, so every author and artist here died before 1956. US status comes from publication before 1931 (or the work being a US public-domain edition).
- **Standard Ebooks** releases its own production work (markup, editing, cover design, image processing) under the [CC0 1.0 Universal dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); the underlying texts and artwork are public domain. Their EPUBs are committed unchanged, so their SHA-256 can be checked against a fresh download.
- **Project Gutenberg** texts are public domain. The Project Gutenberg licence and trademark apply only to copies distributed under the Project Gutenberg name, and its royalty terms only if you charge. We keep PG files unmodified with their header and licence, and only give them away (Alice is free in the demo store). Before selling or modifying a PG file, remove the PG header, footer and licence (or switch to the Standard Ebooks edition).
- **LibriVox** recordings are dedicated to the public domain. They are not committed; a script downloads them (see `fixtures/audio/README.md`).
- **Our own work** (scripts, seed data, the hand-made EPUBs and their sources, the demo serial, its cover) is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
- **AI-generated content** is labelled. _The Lamplighter's Ledger_ was written by an AI (Claude) for this proof of concept; its OPF metadata, a front-matter page, the seed record (`aiUse: "generated"`) and the cover all say so.
- **Fonts**: no font files are committed. The serial's cover was drawn with Liberation Sans (SIL Open Font License 1.1), which allows using the font to make images. Standard Ebooks covers use League Spartan and Sorts Mill Goudy (SIL OFL). Arial Rounded MT is never bundled (AGENTS.md).
