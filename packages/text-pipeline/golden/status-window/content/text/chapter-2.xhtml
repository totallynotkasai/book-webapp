Carved under the rune was a small square sign, the kind millers once used to mark their sacks.

Mara pressed her palm to the sign. Nothing happened, which was, in its way, a kind of answer.

A scrap of paper by the door read “open me”, in handwriting that was not hers.

She folded the paper into her pocket.
