Chapter 1: Status

The bell over the guildhall door rang once, and the air in front of Mara’s face turned the colour of deep water. She blinked. The colour stayed. Letters were floating in it.

[System] Welcome, Mara Quill. Your status is now visible.

“Status,” she whispered, because that was what the old stories said you should do. The water-coloured square unfolded into a box with a border like frost on a window.

STATUS

Name: Mara Quill

Class: Cartographer (Common)

Level: 1 (0 / 100 XP)

Title: None

StatBaseBonusTotal
Strength4+04
Dexterity7+18
Wits11+213
Endurance6+06
Unspent points3

A cartographer. Not a knight, not a mage. Mara thought about being disappointed and decided to be curious instead. She touched the word Cartographer, and a second, smaller box slid out from under the first.

SKILLS

  • Survey (Level 1)
    • Reveal the shape of a room you stand in.
    • Cost: 2 Wits per use.
      1. Halved in daylight.
      2. Doubled underground.
  • Ink Sense (Level 1)
  • Locked: Chart the Unknown (requires Level 5)

Below the boxes, pinned to the guildhall noticeboard, someone had drawn a map of the lower town. It was wrong in three places. Mara could see that now, as clearly as if the mistakes were glowing.

[System] Quest offered: Correct the Map (0 / 3). Reward: 40 XP. Accept? [Yes] [No]

She said yes. Of course she said yes.
